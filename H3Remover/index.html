Method

Learning what to remove and what to keep

H3Remover adapts a pretrained video editor using reinforcement learning, without paired object-free training targets. Depth rewards encourage the editor to reveal what lies behind the target and preserve surrounding geometry. Appearance and preference rewards help retain unrelated scene details.
